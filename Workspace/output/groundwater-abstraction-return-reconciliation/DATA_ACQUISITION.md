# Data acquisition — Groundwater Abstraction Return Reconciliation

## What the corpus is made of

Two ingredients, declared separately and honestly.

1. **Real measured background.** USGS NWIS *instantaneous values* for parameter
   code `72019` (depth to water level, feet below land surface). These are the
   real fluctuations a groundwater monitoring well records: barometric
   response, earth tides, recharge events and ambient regional pumping.
2. **Simulated abstraction physics.** Confined-aquifer Theis drawdown evaluated
   with the exponential integral, superposed in time over piecewise-constant
   rate blocks and in space over several boreholes. The abstraction scenes, the
   aquifer parameters and the declaration errors are generated by `generate.py`.

Nothing generated is presented as measured, and nothing measured is altered
beyond the conditioning recorded below.

## Conditioning applied to the real series

- resampled to a uniform 3-hour grid, 224 samples (28 days), nearest sample
  within two steps, short gaps linearly interpolated;
- feet converted to metres and sign-flipped so the series reads like head;
- quadratic trend removed, leaving the fluctuation residual;
- a well is kept only when the residual standard deviation is between 0.004 m
  and 1.5 m and no single excursion exceeds 12 standard deviations + 0.05 m.

Measured yield: **1,370 sites seen, 548 kept.** Residual standard deviation
p10 0.0078 m, median 0.0258 m, p90 0.2870 m.

## Background families and the split

The 548 conditioned wells are partitioned once, deterministically, into pool A
(394 wells) and pool B (154 wells). A scene draws its background only from its
own pool, and `prepare.py` sends pool A to train and pool B to test, so **no
real monitoring well contributes background to both splits.**

Each observation series mixes three distinct wells from its pool, each
independently time-warped (factor 0.80–1.25), circularly shifted, optionally
time-reversed and sign-flipped, then combined with random weights in
[0.55, 1.0] and rescaled. This is what defeats the source-retrieval attack
recorded in `SHORTCUT_AND_HEADROOM_AUDIT.md`.
